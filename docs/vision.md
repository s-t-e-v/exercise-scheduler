# Vision

This app helps learners systematically practice textbook exercises through spaced repetition.

The initial target is Arabic textbook exercises from the Zad curriculum.

Users solve exercises offline using their physical or digital textbooks, then self-evaluate their performance in the app.

The product prioritizes:
- low friction
- long-term consistency
- progressive mastery

The app should avoid overwhelming the learner and encourage sustainable long-term practice habits.

The app intentionally avoids unnecessary complexity and gamification in its early versions.
